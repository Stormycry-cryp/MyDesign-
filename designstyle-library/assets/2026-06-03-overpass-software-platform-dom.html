        Frequently Asked
        Questions

        Overpass is an online talent marketplace and payment platform for freelance sales contractors. Business owners and managers use Overpass to interview, hire, and pay experienced sales and support talent.

        We make hiring easier by providing a pool of pre-vetted, qualified, remote contractors, and automatically showing you the reps who best match your needs.

        We make interviewing easier by offering an easy system to connect with potential candidates via phone or video, no matter where they're located.

        We make paying your reps easier by tracking your teams' hours, providing you with a weekly report and invoice, and handling all the sticky details of payment processing.

        Finally, we make it easier to maintain your team and navigate turnover. When you need to fill a vacancy, just bring a new contractor into your existing setup.

        The first step is to create your free account at www.overpass.com. After you've enrolled, one of our Growth Consultants will contact you to learn more about your business, better understand your goals, and provide guidance through the hiring process.

        Overpass acts as a payment facilitator, but officially these reps are freelance sale contractors hired by your company. Our team helps with billing, onboarding, and technical support, but it is your responsibility to develop a professional relationship with your team, and ensure they have the resources and product knowledge to effectively represent your company.

        Our Customer Success team is always happy to help troubleshoot technical issues, or offer guidance.

        During office hours (Monday - Friday, 9:00 - 5:00 Eastern time) you can reach us instantly by simply clicking the blue “chat” icon which appears in the lower corner of every Overpass page. We’re also reachable by phone at (718) 360-9450 or by emailing help@overpass.com.
        If you need help after our office hours, we have a friendly “chat-bot” who can help resolve the most common problems, or just leave us a message and we’ll respond first thing the next day.

        Our story began in 2013, and we began working with our earliest clients in 2016. Our first office was located in Newark, NJ, and we've since expanded to Brooklyn, NY and Romania -- plus we employ remote workers from all over the world!
