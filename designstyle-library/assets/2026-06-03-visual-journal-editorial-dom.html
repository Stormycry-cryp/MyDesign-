Visual Journal

Hello! I’m Alessandro Scarpellini, art director and design manager from Italy with a reductive and modernist visual language, specialized in strategy, branding, and visual communication. Visual Journal is my blog, where I showcase the finest branding and graphic design projects from around the world, selected through careful, personal research.

If you’re a designer or studio with a project you believe may fit Visual Journal, feel free to reach out at aesse@alessandroscarpellini.it. Please share the complete case study via a Drive or Dropbox folder, clearly organized into two sections:
. web publication
. instagram, including the relevant formats

All submissions are reviewed editorially on a case-by-case basis, and as part of my commitment to quality and independence, Visual Journal remains free from ads and sponsorships; if you’d like to support the project’s sustainability, you’re welcome to make a small contribution on my Buy Me A Coffee page.

You can follow the Visual Journal on Instagram, Twitter, Tumblr and LinkedIn
