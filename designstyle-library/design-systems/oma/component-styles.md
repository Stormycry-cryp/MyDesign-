## Card

### Style Evidence
- white slabs on top of full-bleed imagery
- single strong red border on the story card; otherwise almost no border language
- flat UI surfaces; materiality comes from the photograph
- almost entirely rectangular, no ornamental rounding
- no visible shadow grammar; depth comes from photography rather than UI effects

### Content Samples
