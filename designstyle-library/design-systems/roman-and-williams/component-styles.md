## Card

### Style Evidence
- warm off-white shell against soft photographic surfaces
- nearly no visible border language in the accepted viewport
- flat interface surfaces; material richness is expressed through image texture and typography
- flat rectangles, no pill buttons, no ornamental chrome in the first viewport
- little to no shadow; depth comes from photography and image blur

### Content Samples
- hero plus follow-up image strip; the system reads as chapters rather than cards
