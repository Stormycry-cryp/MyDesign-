# Style Reference: A24

## Essence
A cultural/editorial commerce style where restraint lets films, stills, merchandise, and writing carry the mood. The reusable idea is taste through curation: minimal UI, strong media, and a publication-like rhythm.

## When To Use
- Film, music, games, AVG worlds, artist portfolios, cultural magazines, or merchandise shops with strong visual assets.
- Brands where audience identity and taste matter more than feature explanation.
- Narrative pages that should feel collected, not salesy.

## When Not To Use
- SaaS pages needing clear product education and conversion funnels.
- Products without high-quality stills, covers, posters, or editorial material.
- Work tools where users need quick operation instead of mood.

## Visual System
- Layout: Minimal nav, editorial grids, large stills/posters, shop/article modules, generous white space.
- Typography: Simple sans or editorial serif/sans pairing depending section; copy is understated.
- Color: Neutral page foundation; color comes from film stills, covers, and merchandise photography.
- Density: Alternates sparse hero/editorial space with dense catalog grids.
- Shape: Mostly rectangular media; little UI ornament.
- Shadow/depth: Flat editorial composition; depth is photographic.

## Assets
- Image style: Film stills, posters, covers, product photography, behind-the-scenes imagery.
- Illustration/icon style: Minimal; brand marks and small commerce icons.
- Texture/pattern: Print/editorial texture when assets provide it, not UI backgrounds.
- Likely sources or production method: Stills, editorial photography, poster art, CMS-managed media catalog.

## Motion
- Page transitions: Simple fades or instant editorial navigation.
- Micro-interactions: Hover image/title reveals, shop item state, media playback controls.
- Scroll/entrance behavior: Let media appear naturally; avoid over-animated culture pages.
- Timing/easing: Restrained, almost invisible.

## Interaction And Components
- Navigation: Clear split between films, shop, membership/news/editorial areas.
- Buttons/links: Text links and understated commerce CTAs.
- Cards/sections: Media cards should preserve aspect ratio and title metadata.
- Forms/inputs: Newsletter/shop checkout forms should stay simple.
- Feedback states: Sold out, pre-order, screening date, media loading, article states.

## Implementation Notes
- CSS/layout primitives: Editorial grids, masonry/catalog layouts, aspect-ratio media cards, CMS-driven collections.
- Token ideas: Neutral paper/ink palette, poster-grid gap, media title scale, minimal border tokens.
- Libraries or techniques: Image CDN, CMS, lazy loading, lightbox/video components.
- Performance/accessibility concerns: Media-heavy pages need alt text, lazy loading, and preserved layout boxes.

## Borrow
- Let curated assets define the emotional palette.
- Use restraint to make the brand feel confident.
- For AVG, treat key art, character stills, and scene thumbnails as the visual system.

## Avoid Copying
- Do not imitate A24's exact cultural tone without the content quality to support it.
- Avoid making navigation too cryptic in the name of taste.
- Do not use stock-looking imagery; this style collapses without authentic assets.
