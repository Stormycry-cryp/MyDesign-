# Style Reference: Amala Beauty


## Essence
A soft spa-natural layout that pairs product still life with a calm text panel and ritual language. The reusable idea is natural clinical proof softened through stone, cream, copper, and symmetrical split composition.

## When To Use
- Natural skincare with spa credibility and product duos/rituals.
- Pages where a single offer needs to feel calm, premium, and not discount-led.
- Brands with warm neutral product photography and concise ritual copy.

## When Not To Use
- High-drama editorial brands.
- Minimal object-led personal care.
- Science-heavy pages that need data tables and clinical stats.

## Borrow
- Use a strict split when the product still-life and ritual copy are equally important.
