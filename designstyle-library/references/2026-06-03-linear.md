# Style Reference: Linear

## Essence
A quiet professional style for tools people use every day: dark or low-glare surfaces, sharp hierarchy, restrained glow, and product UI as the main proof. The reusable idea is intensity without noise.

## When To Use
- Dashboards, issue trackers, planning tools, team workflow products, or AI copilots embedded in work surfaces.
- Interfaces where users need to scan lists, states, ownership, and progress repeatedly.
- Product pages that should feel premium to builders without looking like a marketing spectacle.

## When Not To Use
- Youthful consumer products that need warmth, humor, or broad lifestyle imagery.
- E-commerce pages where product photography must dominate.
- Beginner onboarding screens that require more visual explanation and hand-holding.

## Visual System
- Layout: Centered narrative with product screenshots nested into dark spatial frames; interior app layouts use strict list/detail and sidebar patterns.
- Typography: Compact modern sans, high precision labels, short claims, small uppercase or muted metadata used sparingly.
- Color: Charcoal, off-white, muted grays, subtle blue/violet glows; accents are stateful and scarce.
- Density: Medium-to-high; content feels organized through alignment and subdued separators.
- Shape: Small radii, thin strokes, refined pills, precise panels.
- Shadow/depth: Soft glows and inner borders imply depth; avoid bulky drop shadows.

## Assets
- Image style: Real app screenshots, changelog snippets, command menus, roadmap/list surfaces.
- Illustration/icon style: Thin geometric icons, monochrome or muted line work.
- Texture/pattern: Fine noise, radial glow, hairline grids, dark gradients.
- Likely sources or production method: Product screenshots composited in Figma; CSS radial gradients and border overlays.

## Motion
- Page transitions: Minimal; continuity matters more than spectacle.
- Micro-interactions: Hover reveals, command palette transitions, list item state changes, subtle focus rings.
- Scroll/entrance behavior: Product panels may fade/slide gently; avoid parallax overload.
- Timing/easing: Fast, precise, 120-220ms for controls; slightly longer for section reveals.

## Interaction And Components
- Navigation: Product-first nav with clear docs/pricing/product routes.
- Buttons/links: Small, precise, strong focus states; primary CTAs should not shout.
- Cards/sections: Cards are app-like panels with borders, not decorative tiles.
- Forms/inputs: Keyboard-friendly, command-palette compatible, dense validation.
- Feedback states: Loading, syncing, completed, blocked, assigned states should be visible and subdued.

## Implementation Notes
- CSS/layout primitives: CSS grid/flex, sticky sidebars, border tokens, dark theme variables, layered radial-gradient backgrounds.
- Token ideas: `surface-0/1/2`, `border-subtle`, `text-muted`, `accent-focus`, compact spacing scale.
- Libraries or techniques: Radix/shadcn-like primitives can fit if styled with restraint; Framer Motion for command/menu transitions.
- Performance/accessibility concerns: Dark UIs need contrast audits; small labels require minimum readable sizes and visible focus.

## Borrow
- Treat the product UI as the visual centerpiece.
- Make density feel calm through alignment, muted dividers, and consistent spacing.
- Use motion to confirm state changes, not to entertain.

## Avoid Copying
- Do not copy Linear's exact dark/glow look for every SaaS; it can become cold quickly.
- Avoid fake productivity screenshots with illegible text.
- Do not hide primary user tasks behind aesthetic minimalism.
