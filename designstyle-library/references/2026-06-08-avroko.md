# Style Reference: AvroKO

## Essence
AvroKO pairs soft museum-like whitespace with a dramatic hospitality image band and elegant serif messaging. The transferable idea is a split emotional register: literary headline on the left, third-party praise on the right, then a rich project image immediately underneath to prove the claim.

## When To Use
- Use for interior design, hospitality, or high-craft studios that need to balance authority with warmth.
- Use when the homepage should feel editorial and spacious rather than hard-selling.
- Use when a project portfolio needs to feel materially rich without becoming visually crowded.

## When Not To Use
- Do not use for fast-growth SaaS, dashboard products, or pages that require dense information above the fold.
- Do not use when the target brand lacks strong imagery; this system depends on high-quality project photography.
- Do not use when the tone should feel playful, loud, or highly interactive.
