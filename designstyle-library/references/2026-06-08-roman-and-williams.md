# Style Reference: Roman and Williams

## Essence
Roman and Williams blends studio storytelling with luxury-commerce infrastructure: a quiet cream header, serif-led hero copy, and cinematic lifestyle media sitting inside a Shopify-backed shell. The reusable lesson is how to make a commerce-adjacent design brand feel editorial and cultured rather than transactional.

## When To Use
- Use for design brands, studios, or cultural commerce sites that need product, practice, and editorial narratives to coexist.
- Use when a quiet cream palette, serif hero copy, and image-led storytelling should carry more emotional weight than overt selling.
- Use for homepages that need to feel crafted, literary, and materially rich.

## When Not To Use
- Do not use for fast utility products, dashboards, or startup landing pages that need compressed explanation and repetitive calls to action.
- Do not use when the information architecture should feel minimal or singular; this reference relies on category branching.
- Do not use to justify clutter from account/cart mechanics; the top shell is only successful when kept calm.
