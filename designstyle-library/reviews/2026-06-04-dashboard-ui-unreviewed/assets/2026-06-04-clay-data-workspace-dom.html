Every GTM data point imaginable, in one place

Understand your total market, your customers, and the gap between them. Then act on it.

  • Enrichments: Access premium data from 150+ providers: identify new leads, score accounts and contacts, and personalize outreach.
  • Intent signals: Take instant action when prospects change jobs, visit your website, mentions your company online, and more.
  • AI-led research: Search public databases, navigate gated forms, and find unique datapoints with Claygent.

AI that’s contextual, consistent, and scalable

Create and scale context-aware AI agents, and launch GTM workflows effortlessly.

  • From idea to workflow: Chat with Sculptor for GTM idea generation, analysis, and table building.
  • Build Claygents to scale: Build and version multiple AI agents and reuse them across workflows for consistency at scale.
  • Bring your own context: Connect Claygent to any MCP server - from Salesforce, to Gong, to Google Docs, to enrich with deeper business context.
3x
3x’d data coverage on contact information, firmographics, and more.

Orchestrate and act on your data, at scale

Bring together enrichments, intent signals, and CRM data, decide who to go after, and trigger action automatically.

  • Act on your data at scale: Sync millions of CRM records with signals from 150+ providers.
  • Build dynamic audiences: Combine signals, enrichment, and CRM data and create high-intent buyer segments.
  • Time outreach perfectly: Alert reps, update your CRM, or launch outreach campaigns in Clay’s native sequencer.

Turn data into action with flexible, iterable workflows

Getting the right data is only half the battle: the real value comes from putting it into action. Connect your data to any tool in your tech stack to launch any GTM use case. Iterate and improve quickly.

AI formatting

Clean and format data with AI in seconds

Use AI to transform any record into the format you need. Concatenate data, ensure consistent formatting, and remove extra emojis, symbols, or unnecessary info from lists.

AI conditional logic

Run action steps conditionally — no engineering needed

Conditionally run workflows based on any logic. Use different providers for different companies, enrich only your best-fit leads, or build in fallbacks (e.g. looking for mobiles only if you don't find emails.).

Destinations

Constantly update any tool — CRM, email sequencer, website builder, or more

Push enriched data to your CRM, data warehouse, email tool, website CMS, ad manager, etc—on a recurring basis. Use our HTTP API capability to build your own integrations without code.
