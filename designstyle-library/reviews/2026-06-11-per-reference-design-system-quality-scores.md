## Rubric

| Dimension | Points | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| Retained component evidence | 24 | Component JSON exists, has broad live-browser samples, and comes from real rendered DOM without retaining raw DOM snapshots in the library. |
| Component coverage | 12 | Captures several useful categories such as nav, buttons, cards, forms, icons, and sections. |
| Design-system retention | 12 | Keeps tokens, palette, moodboard, component-styles, palette colors, and component system categories. |
| Component-style usefulness | 12 | `component-styles.md`/`tokens.json` contain reusable computed CSS, geometry, spacing, type, radii, borders, shadows, and transitions. |
| Interaction states | 8 | Hover/focus attempts and actual computed deltas are captured when observable. |
| Motion structure | 10 | `motion.json` contains structured selector roles, triggers, properties, timing, easing, descriptions, and source evidence. |
| Apply pack | 10 | `tokens.json` has apply-layer tokens and generated `variables.css`, `tailwind.theme.json`, and `motion-presets.css`. |
| Retrieval/validation | 8 | Reference has complete L1/L2/L3 paths, evidence limits, and design-system metadata. |
| Hygiene | 4 | No blocked/challenge text and no abnormal scientific-notation px noise in active evidence. |
